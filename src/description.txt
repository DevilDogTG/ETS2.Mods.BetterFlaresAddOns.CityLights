[orange]DriveDogs: Better Flares City Lights

[normal]An add-on for Better Flares v4.9 - City Lights Addon. Street, tunnel and other environment lights
keep the Better Flares look (flare sprites, colour and beam shape) but light the world about as brightly as
the base game, and the lights Better Flares leaves untouched get the same look, so night driving has one
consistent style.
--------------------
[orange]What changes
[normal]- Brighter environment lights: Better Flares City Lights makes nights much darker (about 30% of
  the base game's light). This mod gives every lamp back its own base-game brightness while keeping
  Better Flares' sprite, colour and beam shape.
- Lights that are missing with City Lights on game 1.62 come back: its files predate 1.62 and drop the
  newer fill lights used in underground depots, player garages and some bus stations. Four orange
  roadwork / sign blinkers and the white tram signal on red, missing with Better Flares Base, come back
  too.
- Environment flares Better Flares leaves vanilla get its sprites where it has one for that colour:
  speed-camera flashes, plane / obstruction lights (white, red, green) and green lamps. Blue beacons and
  coloured or decorative lamps (Akureyri pier, Genoa bridge, coloured omni lights) stay as in the base game.

What does NOT change:
- Headlights and vehicle lights (see DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility).
- Traffic-light timing and lenses (see DriveDogs World).
--------------------
[orange]Requirements / load order
[normal]Top = highest priority in Mod Manager:
1. DriveDogs: Better Flares City Lights   <- this mod
2. DriveDogs: Better Flares More Visibility (optional)
3. Better Flares - Traffic Addon (optional)
4. Better Flares - Headlights.2 (optional)
5. Better Flares - Headlights.1 (optional)
6. Better Flares - City Lights Addon v4.9
7. Better Flares - Base v4.9.1

Only for Euro Truck Simulator 2 1.62 with Better Flares v4.9 / v4.9.1. Better Flares Base and City
Lights Addon are both required. Use the plain City Lights Addon, not "City Lights Addon with Fog".
